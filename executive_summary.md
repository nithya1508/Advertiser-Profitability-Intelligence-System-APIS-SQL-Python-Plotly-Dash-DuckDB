## Strategic Context

Google's commercial growth in EMEA depends not just on revenue volume, but on the **quality and durability** of advertiser investment. This analysis applies the Ads Value Accelerator (AVA) framework to evaluate whether Google's commercial portfolio is generating sustainable, incremental value — or concentrating risk in a narrow set of high-spend, low-retention accounts.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Revenue Concentration Risk (Pareto)
The top 20% of advertisers generate approximately **74% of total revenue**. While Pareto concentration is expected, the long tail (bottom 40%) shows ROAS below break-even, representing a capital allocation inefficiency.

**Implication:** Resource allocation should be tilted toward Tier 1 (Star) and Tier 2 (Growth) advertisers, while systematic AVA intervention is deployed for Tier 4–5 accounts.

### 2. Incrementality vs. Gross ROAS Gap
Across all verticals, **gross ROAS overstates Google's true contribution by 30–45%** once holdout baseline conversions are subtracted. Finance vertical shows the largest gap, suggesting high baseline conversion intent regardless of Google spend.

**Implication:** Commission structures and investment cases should be grounded in iROAS, not gross ROAS, to avoid rewarding baseline revenue.

### 3. Cohort Decay in Q1 Acquisitions
Advertisers acquired in Q1 2023 show a H2:H1 revenue ratio of **0.72** — meaning revenue in months 6–12 is lower than months 1–6. This indicates onboarding-driven spend that does not convert to habitual Google investment.

**Implication:** Product Finance should flag this cohort for CAC/LTV reassessment and collaborate with Sales on retention programming.

### 4. At-Risk Portfolio
**12% of the advertiser base** meets at least two risk criteria (ROAS < 2x, declining trajectory, >60 days inactive). Combined historical revenue at risk: **£2.3M**.

**Recommended Actions:**
- **Win-back:** 3 high-value churned advertisers (>£50K historical rev) warrant immediate AM escalation
- **AVA deep-dive:** 8 accounts flagged HIGH priority — schedule within 2 weeks
- **Scale candidates:** 5 accounts show ROAS >4x but spend <£5K/month — growth conversation warranted

## Methodology Notes

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| 90-day rolling ROAS over daily ROAS | Smooths campaign seasonality; aligns with planning cycles |
| Holdout lift for incrementality | Isolates Google's marginal contribution from baseline intent |
| Cohort LTV over point-in-time revenue | Reveals compounding vs. decaying relationships |
| Z-score anomaly detection | Personalised signal per advertiser vs. global thresholds |

## Recommended KPI Framework

| KPI | Definition | Target | Review Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blended ROAS | Revenue / Spend | ≥ 3.5x | Monthly |
| iROAS | Incremental Revenue / Spend | ≥ 2.5x | Quarterly |
| 90-day Retention Rate | % advertisers active at 90 days | ≥ 70% | Quarterly |
| Cost per Incremental Conversion | Spend / Incremental Conversions | ≤ £45 | Monthly |
| At-Risk Revenue (£) | Revenue at risk from flagged accounts | Minimise | Weekly |
